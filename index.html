About Me

I am a PhD Research Scholar in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi (IIT Delhi), working under the guidance of Prof. Keerti Choudhary. My research focuses on algorithms and graph theory, with a particular interest in fault-tolerant graphs and efficient algorithms for maintaining graph properties in the presence of failures. My current work explores algorithmic techniques for designing efficient and resilient graph structures, particularly in the context of fault-tolerant shortest paths and related graph problems. I am interested in developing theoretical solutions that provide both strong guarantees and efficient computational performance.

Research Mission

Build robust graph algorithms that stay reliable even under multiple network failures.

Current Focus

Fault-tolerant shortest paths, resilient structures, and algorithmic guarantees.

Collaboration Style
